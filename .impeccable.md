## Design Context

### Users
Product owners and small teams looking at anonymous usage events to decide what to improve. A visitor should be able to explore sample data without understanding analytics infrastructure.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, candid. Show measured facts and clearly distinguish sample data from newly ingested data.

### Aesthetic Direction
Approved suite direction: calm, readable analytics. English by default with a complete Chinese toggle. A light ledger-like workspace with generous whitespace, ink headings, quiet green accents and thin dividers. Use a horizontal product navigation and a large trend chart, not the feedback board shell. No decorative gradients, oversized marketing headlines, fake growth claims, or developer copy in normal analytics views. Integration instructions belong in Connect.

### Design Principles
1. Date, environment and application scope remain visible above every metric.
2. Empty results are valid; never invent data to fill a graph.
3. Label metrics as devices and sessions, never verified people.
4. Keep controls stable during typing; query only on submit or explicit selection.
5. Charts have readable axes and equivalent tables; narrow screens preserve every action.
